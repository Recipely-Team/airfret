repository. It does not grant any right to the project's name, brand or visual
identity — copyright licences never do, and this file states the reservation
explicitly so nobody has to guess.

## Not licensed

- The name **Airfret**, and any confusingly similar name.
- The Airfret logo, favicon and instrument icons.
- The domain `airfret.vercel.app` and any domain the project later uses.

You may not use these to publish, distribute or market a build of this software,
whether modified or not. A fork must carry its own name and its own icon.

## What you may do

Everything the AGPL allows: read, run, modify, self-host and redistribute the
code, provided your version is released under the AGPL too and you make its
source available to anyone who uses it over a network. Attribution to this
repository is welcome but is not what this file is about.

## What the code licence does not cover

The recorded instrument samples in `public/samples/`, the scores the real pieces
are transcribed from, and the reference recording the tests download each keep
their own licence, listed in [`public/samples/CREDITS.md`](public/samples/CREDITS.md).
The AGPL does not relicense them.

## Questions

If you want to use the brand for something — a write-up, a talk, a fork you want
to discuss — open an issue and ask. The reservation exists to stop look-alikes,
not to stop people talking about the project.
