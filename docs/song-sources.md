# Song sources and rights

Where each melody that isn't a traditional tune written out by hand came from, and why it may ship.

| Song | Source | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| İstiklal Marşı | Official voice-and-piano score, Presidency of the Republic of Türkiye (tccb.gov.tr, *istiklalmarsi_nota.pdf*); transcribed by hand and checked bar by bar against the SymbTr encoding (used only to check) | Decree 2010/1126, Resmî Gazete 27780 (9 Dec 2010): outside the economic rights of Law 5846; nobody may charge for reproducing or performing it. Public domain in the US (published 1930). Played as written (TCK 300) |
| Üsküdar'a Gider İken (Kâtibim) | Wikimedia Commons, *File:Katibim.svg* (LilyPond source), read from the rendered score | CC0 1.0; anonymous, 19th century |
| İzmir Marşı | Wikimedia Commons, *File:Izmir.mid* (one stanza); its four-stanza form from the public-domain recording *File:İzmir'in Dağlarında (Takabeg).ogg* (the same 16-second stanza four times) | CC BY-SA 4.0 (the arrangement); the tune is anonymous / public domain |
| Gençlik Marşı | Otto Lindblad, *Längtan till landet* ("Vintern rasat", 1839) — Swedish Musical Heritage edition no. 2127, Royal Swedish Academy of Music 2019 (swedishmusicalheritage.com); the top voice, the bass staff's chord roots | Public domain (stated on the edition; Lindblad d. 1864). The Turkish words (Ali Ulvi Elöve) are not used |
| Genç Osman Türküsü | Wikimedia Commons, *File:Ballad of Genç Osman.mid*, quantised to triplets | CC0 1.0; anonymous, 17th century |

Not included: *Onuncu Yıl Marşı* (Cemal Reşit Rey, d. 1985 — protected until 2055); anything from SymbTr (CC BY-NC-SA, incompatible with this project's AGPL-3.0).
